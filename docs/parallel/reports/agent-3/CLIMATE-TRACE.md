Quantities are tonnes of CO2, not CO2 equivalent; no facility positions or ownership are included. The power sector contains other subsectors, so these are not total power or national emissions. The [v5.12.0 electricity-generation methodology](https://github.com/climatetracecoalition/emissions-methodology/tree/main/Sector-Methodologies/1-Sectors/power/electricity-generation) describes modeled plant estimates, including reported generation where available, satellite and machine-learning estimates, baseline estimates, and emission factors. Country totals include asset estimates and additional plant estimates. Estimates can be revised and should not be interpreted as directly observed emissions.

[Terms](https://climatetrace.org/terms) license Climate TRACE emissions and metadata under CC BY 4.0 with attribution and changes indicated, except listed external datasets. Country-level **other energy use** is an EDGAR exception; the query selects only electricity-generation, which is not among the exceptions listed there. The capture records the methodology's recommended citation and identifies normalization as an adaptation. It retains country identifiers but no GADM boundary data. Other subsectors must be reviewed separately.
