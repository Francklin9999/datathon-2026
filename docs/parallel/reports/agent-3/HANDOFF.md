Every new capture is bounded, manually retrieved on 2026-11-15, and cannot be presented as available in earlier historical replay. NASA POWER is 186 gridded daily values at six requested points, not station measurements. EPA SEMS is a 438-record site-reference sample, not emissions or current contamination. Federal Register, ECB and UK sanctions rows are publication metadata and have no source point coordinates. USGS minerals, trade and CPI values are country or global records without invented points; CPI levels are not inflation rates. The monthly CPI and Climate TRACE dates are labeled reference periods. The new BLS CPI is one U.S. monthly index series; Wikimedia pageviews are topic attention counts without reader geography; WGI political stability is an annual composite estimate; and CMR polygons are satellite product coverage, not fire detections. The reports beside this handoff state each selected slice, attribution and reuse terms.
