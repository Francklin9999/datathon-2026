The standard immutable snapshot loader verified normalized and all raw checksums in the staged evidence directory, loaded six unique rows from three study IDs, and confirmed Canadian country identity and unset display coordinates. An as-of query at `2026-11-01T00:00:00Z` returned zero rows, confirming capture-time gating. No tests were added or run.

Reproduce with `PYTHONPATH=backend .venv/bin/python -m aplomb.world.providers.trials_extra --directory docs/evidence/parallel/agent-3/trials-extra/providers`.
