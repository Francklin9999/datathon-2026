# Remaining miscellaneous official sources

Completed the anonymous NY Fed Survey of Consumer Expectations integration for SRC-102. The immutable official workbook supplies twelve 2024 monthly U.S. median one-year-ahead inflation expectations. Values retain the source percent unit; they are household survey expectations, not measured CPI inflation. National observations have country `USA` and no point coordinates. Source title `Inflation expectations` and the series title remain unchanged.

Adapter: `backend/aplomb/world/providers/misc_official.py`. Capture: `world_nyfed_sce_inflation_2024-2026-11-15-4e76b21cc940394d`, beneath `backend/aplomb/world/bundle/providers/`. The adapter uses the standard library to parse only the selected statistical sheet and embedded License/Disclaimer sheets, validates series identity, all twelve monthly periods, finite numeric values, duplicates and source licence wording, and bounds download size to 2 MB and XML expansion to 12 MB per member. Fetches allow at most two attempts with 20-second socket/read limits, fixed official URLs and no redirects. Capture publication preserves the original workbook, official SCE page and general terms under SHA-256 checksums, and gates availability at retrieval.

The workbook's dataset-specific licence explicitly grants worldwide royalty-free reproduction, distribution and derivative works. Its prescribed source attribution, copyright notice, free source address, modifications disclosure and liability disclaimer accompany the manifest and every runtime observation. The unmodified workbook retains the original licence and source identifiers. No logo or endorsement is used. The general terms additionally require distributed source content to retain its permissions/conditions; the manifest preserves that condition. Dataset rights were established from the embedded licence, rather than assuming the NY Fed inherits Board-produced public-domain status.

Runtime discovery automatically consumes the immutable manifest. One additive branch in `world/service.py::_observation_summary` exposes each month, exact expected inflation rate, percent unit, survey interpretation and prescribed attribution while preserving the original source title. No shared catalogue, passport registry, ingestion dispatcher or contract files were edited. Root integration must register `world_nyfed_sce_inflation_2024` in the dataset catalogue/passport registry: source Federal Reserve Bank of New York; source URL `https://www.newyorkfed.org/microeconomics/sce`; licence “FRBNY SCE dataset-specific royalty-free reuse and derivative licence”; licence URL the official workbook's embedded License sheet. The immutable provider manifest includes full normalized rights evidence and attribution.

Reconstruction: `PYTHONPATH=backend .venv/bin/python -m aplomb.world.providers.misc_official`. This explicitly refreshes the source; ordinary runtime reads use saved data only.

Manual validation through the real FastAPI application checks HTTP observation output, monthly reference-period timing, all twelve selected values, absent point locations, and capture-time historical exclusion. The standard loader verifies every saved raw/normalized checksum and row count. Evidence is `docs/evidence/parallel/agent-3/misc-official/runtime-validation.json`. No test files were added and no test suite was run. No commit, push, deployment, login, key, form submission or paid access occurred.
