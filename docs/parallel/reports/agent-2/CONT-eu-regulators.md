# European regulatory sources (Agent 2)

## Implemented and source-backed

- **SRC-052, ESMA Registers:** exact validated LEI → public ESMA authorised entities A2A query, fixed Solr query and field projection, maximum 20 rows, local exact-LEI revalidation, reported total and partial flag. The endpoint is `GET /v1/intelligence/eu/esma-entity/{lei}`. A live local HTTP call on 2026-11-15 returned five records for BNP Paribas LEI `R0MUWSFPU8MPRO8K5P83`; the projected source JSON and SHA-256 are retained in `docs/evidence/parallel/agent-2/eu-regulators/`. It stores immutable raw and normalized captures in the existing session-owned store. A missing row does not prove lack of authorization. Current source capture is not a historical snapshot. ESMA [A2A documentation](https://registers.esma.europa.eu/publication/helpApp) describes `ae_lei` and the public predefined query service. ESMA [registry legal notice](https://registers.esma.europa.eu/publication/legalNoticePage) permits reproduction subject to attribution and conditions, including that free source availability be disclosed in sold documents and no implied ESMA endorsement.
- **SRC-053, EBA EU-wide Transparency Exercise:** acquired the real complete 2025 market-risk CSV, 3,806,290 bytes, 23,817 records and four reporting periods. Its lossless gzip is `backend/aplomb/intelligence/data/eba_2025_market_risk.csv.gz`; SHA-256 of the uncompressed original is `61df1e82d48c0179437e688ac8c3dbdf37000de9b662db0911d8dd917fce84bc`. The endpoint is `GET /v1/intelligence/eu/eba-market-risk/{lei}?period=202506`; it verifies the bundled source hash, filters by exact checksum-valid LEI and reporting period, preserves source row/column and approach/product/risk dimensions, and persists normalized session-owned observations against that source. Actual local HTTP returned 51 source rows for BNP Paribas, 2025-06. This slice is **market risk only**; credit, sovereign and other EBA transparency files are not ingested. EBA publishes its [full database](https://www.eba.europa.eu/risk-and-data-analysis/risk-analysis/eu-wide-transparency-exercise), and the [2025 bank market risk template](https://www.eba.europa.eu/assets/TE2025/Banks_individuals_results/883401/DE_529900S1KHKOEQL5CK20_TR_2025.pdf) identifies units as EUR million. EBA's [legal notice](https://www.eba.europa.eu/find-out-about-us/legal-notice) allows reproduction with attribution subject to third-party rights and exceptions. Its site says the annual transparency exercise was discontinued; this historical capture is not current Pillar 3 data.

Capture record: `docs/evidence/parallel/agent-2/eu-regulators/capture-manifest.json`. EBA source availability is conservatively the capture timestamp `2026-11-15T17:10:59Z`; original dataset publication time is not asserted. Reported bank risk measures are not portfolio holdings or security exposure, and overlapping item/portfolio/product codes are not summed. One aggregate synthetic `xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx` is intentionally excluded by strict LEI validation.
