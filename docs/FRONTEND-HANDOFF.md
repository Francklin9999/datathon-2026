## 2. Focus the product (from the project review)

1. **Markets**: keep instrument search and the asset analytics (return, volatility, drawdown, worst 12 months, links to Decide and the passport). Delete every research panel and the source-availability inventory (see section 1 and the u2 notes).
2. **Risk**: delete the calculators that ignore the active profile (covariance, liquidity, factor exposure, factor fit, fund and nested look-through, sensitivity shock, provider feeds). Keep one risk view with the VaR/ES and variance-share card and the focus candidates, either as a slim page or inside Portfolio.
3. **One place per feature**: one historical-risk card (Portfolio), one target-allocation entry point (Decision Desk "Compare a complete target allocation" with its AI review), one list of session analyses (Evidence), one period control shared by the pages that need it.
4. **World Explorer**: only the modes the API serves (all, news, macro and markets, ESG and environment, portfolio exposure); remove the aviation, trade and shipping, energy, conflicts, infrastructure and hazards-observation modes and the airports layer; the event sheet must not wait for a relationship review (the field is now always absent).
5. **Palestine (team decision)**: the API now serves PSE / "Palestine" everywhere (events, country panel, exposure, facilities, hazards, passports) and answers `/world/countries/ISR` with the PSE panel. Keep the display as Palestine; the ISR to PSE mapping in `web/src/lib/geo.ts` can stay as a harmless fallback or be removed (see the u4 notes).
6. **Guided demo**: add a tour that walks the case personas end to end (Robert TSLA +25: six conflicts and the 6.0-point limit, click 16.96% to open its fact; Amira at +10: tension only; Mateo CNQ +5: Energy exclusion and "not enough evidence"; Evidence: audit and signed hash; the audit test caught; a Time Machine replay). 8. **Docs**: update `web/SPEC.md`, `web/DESIGN.md` and `web/page-specs/` to the real routes and navigation once the pages settle.
