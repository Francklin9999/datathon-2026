# Design system and interaction rules

**Owner:** T13. Direction set on 2026-11-15 by the Liquid Glass redesign (spec `2026-11-15-liquid-glass-design`), which replaces the unslop pass of the same day (spec `2026-11-15-unslop-dashboard-design`); the navigation overhaul of the same day grouped the menu, moved the expert tools to Labs and merged repeated sections, and the frontend handoff of release `8e560ef` then removed the tools whose API routes the backend cleanup retired. Reference: three dashboard captures supplied by the user (Reports, Good morning, Activity), adapted to Aplomb's palette and pages. Every page's kept sections and buttons, with the API they call, the requirements they serve and the grading criterion they support: [UI inventory](../docs/UI-INVENTORY.md).

## Direction

- **Product:** tests hypothetical investment changes against a fictional investor's plan; every figure links to its source.
- **Character:** calm, exact, evidential, light to use.
- **Material:** frosted glass panes floating over a fixed ambient field (fine graph paper, the survey world of the plumb-line logo, under two still pools of cobalt and aqua light).
- **Moment:** the fictional capital is the one cobalt tile; the history chart, the plan verdict and the correlation heatmap carry the colour. The chrome stays graphite.
- **Signature:** a sourced figure has a dotted underline; clicking it opens its source.
- **Less to read:** one useful sentence under each title; notes live behind an "i" (`InfoTip`), in a folded `<details className="fold">` or in one `Note`; technical identifiers (run, request, model, dataset version, hash) only under "Technical details" or on Evidence and System.

## Glass

`.glass` (page panel), `.glass-pop` (popovers) and `.chrome` (floating chrome) paint their fill and `backdrop-filter` on `::before` and a specular rim on `::after`, behind the content in an isolated stacking context, so menus opened inside them can still frost the page. Cards inside the panel are translucent without their own blur. Under `prefers-reduced-transparency`, without `backdrop-filter` support, or in forced colours, every pane falls back to an opaque surface.

## Type and shape

Instrument Sans (variable weight and width, local `@fontsource-variable/instrument-sans`) for everything; IBM Plex Mono only for identifiers in technical details. Titles 650 weight, tight tracking; figures in tabular numerals. Sentence case. Type steps from the scale above: 12 captions, 13 meta, 15 lead, on a 14 px body. Radius by size: panel 28, card 20, a block nested in a card 14, control 12, chip 8; buttons, filter chips and pills fully round. Controls are 40 px high, compact ones 32 px.

**Buttons wrap.** A long label breaks onto balanced lines inside its pill (`white-space: normal`, `text-wrap: balance`, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`); the pill grows in height from its minimum, never past its container's width. Action rows, card actions and the armed `ConfirmButton` pair wrap as whole buttons (`flex-wrap`). French labels therefore never overflow or get cut (`tests/ui-logic.test.ts`).

## Layout

- **Top bar** (floating and fixed, not sticky: typing in a sticky bar's field makes Chrome scroll the page back to the top; on phones it scrolls with the page): investor switcher, **Ask Aplomb** (text sent to Decide as `?q=`, interpreted once; `/` focuses it; where the browser has speech recognition a microphone dictates into the field, one utterance per press, cobalt while listening, Escape cancels; the words are only filled in, never sent on their own; while typing, never for dictated words, a grey completion of a request phrase, ticker or company name follows the caret, accepted with Tab or →, hidden by Escape, built by code from the universe and holdings in `src/lib/completion.ts`), the simulation note (short on the bar, full text for assistive technology), Help (tutorials) and Settings (language when bilingual, theme, animations, data date). "Manage profiles" sits under the investors in the investor switcher (when `profile_creator` is on).
- **Navigation** (floating rail on the left, under the top bar; the menu drawer on phones, below 768 px): the pages in labelled groups, in this order, then a footer (`NAV_GROUPS` in `src/lib/ui/nav.ts`, tested in `tests/nav-groups.test.ts`): **Overview** (Home), **Plan** (Portfolio, Decide, Strategies, Replay), **Explore** (World), **Trust** (Evidence, System), then a hairline and **Sources** and **Labs**. Labels are always visible. The compact rail (92 px) puts a 12 px caption under each icon, up to two lines and never cut, with a hairline between groups; their headings stay for assistive technology. The expanded rail (216 px; the arrow at the bottom, `aplomb.pref.sidebar`, applied before paint as `html[data-sidebar='expanded']`) shows the headings and puts each label beside its icon. Each label is in the DOM once (`.nav-text`, `NavLink.tsx`), so a link's name is its label, and each group's list is named by its heading. Each page has its own icon, never shared (`NavRail.tsx` maps the names of `nav.ts`). One white highlight glides to the current page; it is placed without a glide on load. Replay (`time_machine`) and World (`world_explorer`) are hidden only once `/meta` reports their flag off, so the list does not jump while the app boots; a group left empty disappears. The phone drawer lists the same groups with their headings, then "Manage profiles"; the 404 page lists the same entries flat. Profiles has no rail entry (it opens from the investor switcher). Markets and Risk are gone from the menu; their routes redirect ([SPEC](SPEC.md) §1).
- **Mini globe** (the World entry, `src/components/shell/MiniGlobe.tsx`): a small SVG orthographic globe (projection, graticule, horizon clipping and the design curve in `src/lib/ui/orthographic.ts`, tested in `tests/orthographic.test.ts`), drawn from the site's own `/geo/countries-110m.json`, fetched once per page load. It faces the focus country of `src/state/useFocusCountry.ts`: World's `?country=`, else the listing country of Decide's `?ticker=` (from the asset universe, read only then), else Canada. The country is filled in the accent and also marked by a dot, since small countries vanish at this size; the centre's latitude stays within ±45°, so the globe never looks down on a pole. It turns to a new country once, over 600 ms on the design curve, when animations are allowed, and jumps otherwise; it never spins on its own and is re-projected only during that turn. The link keeps `?country=` when the page named one and reads "World, Canada". From the rail, opening World expands the globe into the World stage with a view transition (shared name `world-globe`, `features/world/world.css`), else with a 160 ms scale and fade. The Earth icon stands in before the address can be read (static render) and when the outlines fail to load. 40 px in the compact rail, 28 px in the expanded rail and the phone menu.
- **Page panel:** title and one sentence on the left, the period control and main action on the right (`PageHeader actions`).
- **Section dock** (floating, bottom): every element in `<main>` with `data-section` and an `id` (via `Card section=…` or a dashboard widget's `section`), shown from three sections, with scroll spy; the cobalt + tests a move. Always visible on desktop, hidden on phones. Its size is `--dock-h` / `--dock-gap` (shell.css); `--dock-clearance` is reserved as the page panel's bottom padding and as the root's `scroll-padding-bottom`, so focus and scroll targets stop above it (zero on phones).
- **Home:** scope bar (period or custom range, benchmark, asset classes, minimum weight, chart view). Only the period reaches the API (every window-based figure); the benchmark adds a line to Past performance; asset classes filter Allocation and Positions, minimum weight Positions only, and both say so under the control. A new custom Range starts from the period on screen. The scope is saved in this browser under `aplomb.pref.dashboard.scope.v1`, shared by every profile, until Reset; when it is not the default a "Custom scope: … · Reset" line above the bar names it and says it is saved in this browser. Below 768 px only the period stays visible and the other filters sit in a "Filters" disclosure, closed on every load. Reset layout sits in the page header, always shown, inactive while the layout is the default over a 12-column grid of widgets (`aplomb.pref.dashboard.layout.v1`); key figures are four tiles with the fictional-capital tile in cobalt (the profile's capital, which the value chart starts from, not a value today). Details: the movable widgets and details sheets spec (`2026-11-15-movable-widgets-details-design`).
  - **Always arrangeable, no edit mode.** Grab a widget by its header: after 5 px of travel it lifts (scale 1.025, denser glass, deeper shadow) and follows the pointer, a recessed slot shows where it will land, the others glide out of its way, and it settles into the slot on release. A plain click on the header stays a click. The grabber at the top edge (on hover or focus) is the keyboard handle (arrows, Home/End); the corner handle resizes within the widget's limits (arrows too). Every change is announced. Below 880 px of grid width the widgets stack and do not move.
  - **A widget never scrolls.** It shows its essentials and adapts to its size: Positions shows the rows that fit, then "+N"; Plan health adds the rules nearest their limit as room allows; the chart fills the widget. Tables, limitations, method, the full brief and its steps open from ⤢ in a details sheet. Minimum sizes still hold the essentials.
- **Portfolio:** four tabs (Holdings, Risk, Rules, Accounts) instead of one long page. The Risk tab is the app's one risk view: historical risk, the tail-loss and variance-share cards of the former Risk page and the deterministic focus candidates. The three rule builders share one "Add a rule" card on the Rules tab.

## Strategies

[Strategies](page-specs/strategies.md) shows standing automations (rebalancing, regular contributions, risk guard) simulated on history next to the untouched holdings. Each kind has one colour, used only for its icon and its action labels: rebalance cobalt, contributions green, risk guard amber. Figures compare with "untouched" in a quiet second line; the strategy's figure turns green or red only when it is clearly better or worse. Home has no strategy widget since the navigation overhaul (the Automations widget was removed; a stored layout that still names it drops it), so each strategy's next action shows on this page only. Proposals only, said in the page's header and under the next action.

## Labs

Labs (`/labs/`, `src/features/labs/`) holds the expert tools that do not belong on a main page, shown one at a time: six tools in three categories, **Instruments** (instrument discovery, from Markets), **Planning** (target drift, the joint goals plan, goal funding, the spending and liquidity scenario, from Portfolio) and **Comparison** (two saved profiles side by side, from Profiles). The overhaul first moved 31 tools here; the research connectors and the risk calculators among them left with their API routes in release `8e560ef`, and the focus candidates moved to Portfolio's Risk tab ([UI inventory](../docs/UI-INVENTORY.md) §16 and §19). The registry (`registry.ts`) is data: an id (the `?tool=` value, default `instruments`, kept with `useTabParam`), a category, a name and a one-line purpose in both languages, an icon and whether the tool reads the selected profile; `tests/labs-registry.test.ts` checks it. No tool waits on a source gate any more. A tool's code loads only when it is opened (`next/dynamic`, no server render).

The list sits on the left (at most 300 px wide, sticky, scrolling on its own): each tool's icon, name and purpose, the current one in the accent (soft cobalt fill and rim, cobalt icon). The panel on the right repeats the tool's icon, name and purpose above the tool. At 900 px and below the list goes above the panel as a grid of tool buttons, and choosing a tool moves focus to the panel heading and scrolls it under the top bar. Tools that read the profile sit behind `ProfileGate` and start afresh for another profile, a saved version or, when their figures depend on it, another period. The header says once that nothing here is advice.

## Guided tours

`src/lib/tour/engine.ts` (placement, mask, progress; tested in `tests/tour-logic.test.ts`), `src/features/tour/` (provider, overlay, help menu, scripts). Steps point at `data-tour="…"` attributes (`tests/tour-targets.test.ts` checks that each exists), open their page, select the tab that holds their target, wait up to 4 s for their element and skip optional ones that never appear. A welcome offers the basic tour once (`aplomb.pref.tour.v1`): investor, Ask Aplomb, the grouped menu, the mini globe, the period, the simulation note, settings and help. The ? button replays it, offers the persona demo tour (Robert with TSLA +25, Amira at +10, Mateo with CNQ +5, the Evidence audit, the audit test and a replay) and lists one short tour per page of the menu, plus Profiles. Keys: arrows, Enter, Escape; focus stays in the bubble and returns afterwards.

## Components

StatusBadge pairs icon and text. FactChip is a button whose accessible name uses `display`. `Listbox` is the APG listbox; `SegmentedControl` a radio group with a gliding ink pill; `InfoTip` an "i" whose note is also the button's description; `RunsTable` the one list of analyses (on Evidence), with state filter chips, each row opening its evidence or its steps (on System). `SuggestInput` is the APG combobox for every field with suggestions (asset in Decide and Replay; country, ticker and country search in World; reporting currency and investor type in Profiles): the universe is read once per data version (`useAssetUniverse`, shared with the Labs instrument search, holdings first and still offered if that read fails) and ranked locally on each key by `src/lib/suggest.ts`, with no request per keystroke and no native `datalist`; clicking a field that is empty or holds one of its values lists them all, like a select. Filter-as-you-type tables (the Labs instrument search, the Sources directory, the fact ledger) prepare their searched text once and follow a deferred query; all of these ignore case and accents. `DonutChart` legends carry bars and every value; `LineChart` keeps legend and units outside the tooltip and has `SeriesTable`. Targets are 44 px (invisible `::after` where smaller); focus ring 2 px `--focus`, 2 px offset. `Card details={{ render }}` adds ⤢ and its `Sheet` (a centred glass panel, a bottom sheet on phones, growing out of the button that opened it). Dialogs (`Drawer`, `Sheet`) share `useDialogFocus`: focus moves in, Tab is trapped, Escape closes, focus returns to the opener, and the page does not scroll behind; open dialogs form a stack, so a source drawer opened from a sheet closes alone. Both render in a portal after mount (fact scopes still apply). `LineChart fill` sizes the chart to its container and `compact` puts the unit in the legend. On phones an `InfoTip` note is a fixed card at the bottom of the screen, so a hidden note can never widen the page.

**Shared primitives** (`src/styles/primitives.css`). `Tabs` (`components/ui/Tabs.tsx`) is the APG tablist with automatic activation: one tab stop, arrows, Home and End move and select, and the same gliding ink pill as `SegmentedControl`, so a section switcher looks like every other choice; the caller renders the active panel with `tabPanelProps` (labelled by its tab, focusable, fading in on change), and `useTabParam` keeps the choice in the address with `history.replaceState` (`?tab=` on Portfolio, `?tool=` on Labs), so a link or a refresh lands on it without a navigation or a scroll jump. `ConfirmButton` (`components/ui/ConfirmButton.tsx`) is a destructive action in two steps: the first press turns it into "Confirm …" beside Cancel and moves focus there; it returns to rest after 5 s, on Escape or when focus leaves the pair (removing a conditional rule, deleting a profile). `Note` (`components/common/Note.tsx`) is the one quiet look for a short caveat or hint inside a card, with its icon, `info` or `caution`; `ProxyNote` is the single wording of the listing-country and quotation-currency caveat that several pages used to repeat. `Redirect` (`components/common/Redirect.tsx`) serves a moved page: the static export has no server redirects, so the old address replaces itself on the client with the new one, keeping its query and hash (`src/lib/redirect.ts`), and shows "This page moved to …" with a link meanwhile.

**Popovers render above the chrome.** `Listbox` and `SuggestInput` lists render in a portal (`portalTarget()`: the full-screen element while one is shown, else `document.body`) as a fixed `.popover-portal`, placed from the trigger's box by `placePopover` (`components/ui/popoverPlacement.ts`, pure, tested in `tests/ui-logic.test.ts`): under the trigger, or above it when it does not fit below and there is more room above; inside the viewport on both axes; never narrower than the trigger (`SuggestInput`: exactly the field's width); at most min(360 px, 60 vh) tall, scrolling inside. `usePopoverPosition` (`components/ui/usePopover.ts`) keeps it in place on scroll, resize and content changes (scrolling inside the list does not move it); `usePresence` keeps it mounted until its exit transition ends, read from its computed style, so closing is immediate when motion is off. Layers: rail and dock 32; top bar 38, whose own menus (simulation note, help, settings) open inside it and so cover the rail and the dock; scrim 40, sheets 45, drawers 48–50, chart tooltip 70, World full-screen stage 1000, portal lists 1010, `InfoTip` bubbles 1020. A list opened in a sheet, a drawer or next to the chrome is never cut off or hidden behind it.

## Motion

One curve and four durations (tokens above). Pills glide (rail, dock, segments, tabs), figures count up from 0 when they appear and from the old value when they change (`CountUp`; screen readers get the final text at once), menus rise 4 px, drawers slide, sheets grow from their button, tab panels fade in, the tour bubble glides between steps, the mini globe turns once to a new country. One entrance on load; dashboard widgets enter through the Web Animations API and glide FLIP-style whenever their position changes (drag, keys, resize, reset), interruptible mid-flight. Browsers restart CSS animations on a node React re-inserts, so in-widget entrances are keyed to fresh content: `data-fresh` on drawn charts (`useFresh`), and on the grid for donuts and bars. Moving a widget never replays them. Nothing loops but the loading spinner.

**One gate** (`src/styles/motion.css`, the only one). Motion stops when Settings > Animations is **Off** (`aplomb.pref.anim`, applied before paint as `html[data-anim]`, `src/lib/ui/theme.ts`), when the page is hidden (`html[data-motion="off"]`, set by the boot script), or when the system asks to reduce motion, unless Animations is **On**, the default (product decision, 2026-11-15); **Auto** follows the system. The gate sets the duration tokens to 0 s and neutralises any duration, delay or stagger written outside them, so `backwards` entrances show their final frame at once; on a hidden page animations are removed instead, so an entrance plays once when the tab is first shown. It covers popovers, buttons, tables and the tour as well as the load animations and the shell. JavaScript motion reads the same three inputs: `useAnimationsAllowed()` (live, for `MotionConfig` in `ThemeProvider`, `Sheet` and `Drawer`), `readAnimationsAllowed()` (`CountUp`) and `animationsAllowed()` (the mini globe); `DashboardGrid` repeats the same rule, and the World globe's rotation follows Animations, then the system setting. `tests/ui-logic.test.ts` checks the gate, and that no shared stylesheet carries its own reduced-motion switch. Still outside the gate: smooth scrolling to a section from the dock, to a tour step and to World's panels follows the system setting alone, and the settings, help and simulation-note popovers and the dictation note drop their entrance under the system setting even when Animations is On (`shell.css`).

## Wording

Use "hypothetical scenario", "rule assumption", "historical in this sample", "financial impact not established" and the boundary label "Largest size before the first rule conflict". Say "analysis", not "run"; "steps", not "execution trace"; "audit test", not "sabotage test". The simulation note is on every page (top bar) and in every brief; a card does not repeat it. No trailing periods on labels and buttons, no exclamation marks, no raw enum or code shown as a label.

## Review

Check both themes, the compact rail (captions on two lines in French) and the expanded rail (headings), the phone menu, the mini globe turning to a country and expanding into World, focus paths (sheet → source drawer → back) and tours by keyboard, that no Home widget scrolls at its default and minimum sizes, header drag (a click stays a click), Labs at 1440 and 900 px, the persona demo tour end to end, lists opened in a sheet and next to the top bar, AA contrast, 375 / 768 / 1024 / 1440 px, Animations On, Auto and Off with and without reduced motion, reduced transparency, long French labels (buttons wrap, nothing overflows), and no-data, error and feature-off states.
